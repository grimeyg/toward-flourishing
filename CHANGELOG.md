# Changelog

A dated record of public updates. For how and why the axioms changed, see [Axiom revisions](axiom-revisions.md).

## October 30, 2026

- **Axioms:** a diagram under Causation traces the chain from the Big Bang to a single thought, marks where chance may enter, and shows two loops back to the present moment: one checked against the world, one against a label. New sections on two kinds of facts (the external world and experience) and on luck, with Nagel's four kinds. Control without authorship now says the same control serves every path; what differs is what it answers to.
- **Framework:** the three paths are described as three ways of running one loop.
- **Questions:** three new open questions. Is anti-conformity a fourth path? Do the non-virtuous paths share a motive, protecting the self-model? Can a system run the virtuous path when no one in it does?
- Smaller wording changes in Embodiment, Emergence, Compression, and the Hypothetical Imperative.

First public version: the framework, the axioms and their revision history, the uses of "good," questions, and who's who.
