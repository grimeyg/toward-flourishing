# Toward a Definition of Flourishing

A working framework for what it means to live well, for a person and for a society.
By defining Justice as a "facilitation of flourishing" we can further argue how positive rights are necessary to facilitate a flourishing society, not just the negative rights many governments provide. The right to clean water, food, housing, and healthcare are all necessary to flourish. Flourishing is chosen as the end because it is never complete, and it's the measure by which we judge all activities we do. 

Revenge and retribution are commonly viewed as a part of Justice, but even in these cases we can see through the lens of flourishing. Sometimes, we can't move forward (flourish) unless the Injustice is addressed sufficiently. To the victim, violence and making the perpetrator feel what they felt or more, is often thought to be Justice; "An eye for an eye". While it may feel like this is the only route forward to flourish, it is often not in reality. Restorative Justice is a way for the victim and perpetrator to understand what led to the event and understand the fallout of the event. Seeing the humanity in each other can ofter reduce the objectification the perpetrator initially saw in the victim, and reduce the fear, trauma, and anger the victim holds. Breaking the cycle of violence is the goal. 

There has to be a floor in society, things we don't allow, and also for ourselves individually lest we sink lower and lower in our character. That being said the framework only holds if one values character, consistency, logical argumentation, and harm reduction. All of those concepts are linked in reality so valuing one implies valuing the others. If someone wants to lean into illogical behavior they can but the framework wont really hold for them.

## The short version

Five states every life faces, three paths for meeting them, one end.

- **Five states.** Three are the world acting on us: precarity, impermanence, and friction. Two are the mind: desire and meaning-making.
- **Three paths.** Felt certainty is consistent only with yourself; conformity, only with others. The virtuous path keeps beliefs open to revision and checks them against the world. (Possibly more paths; anti-conformity)
- **One end.** Flourishing: living well over time, never finished.

The full argument is in [the framework](framework.md). It rests on eight [axioms](axioms.md).
